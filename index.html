Shorts Fabrik

Shorts Fabrik is a small private desktop tool. It is used by one person only: the owner of the YouTube channel Goldenegg.

It uploads YouTube Shorts that the channel owner made to their own channel and schedules when they are published. It uses the YouTube Data API (videos.insert and channels.list) only for this one channel.

  • No other users can sign in or use it.
  • No data is sold, shared or used for ads.
  • Everything runs on the owner's own computer.
